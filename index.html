Unsafe Food Awareness

Issues

MSG

Many Myanmar traditional dishes are healthy, using fresh vegetables, meat, and fish. However, some sellers use Monosodium glutamate as a shortcut, reducing nutritional quality and potentially causing headaches.

Preservatives

Mont phat is made out rice which is healthy but some businesses use preservatives to make the Mont phat last longer which are bad for your health.

Artificial coloring

Some sellers use artificial coloring in the mango salad to make it look better and attract customers. (bad for your kidneys and liver and can cause cancer)

Chemicals

Fruits and vegetables are sometimes contaminated with harmful dyes and chemicals to make them look fresher, greener, or more appealing. This is due to poor regulation and unsafe practices in the food supply chain.

Artificial coloring

Ice salad is made using a variety of recipes, with food coloring playing an important role in adding both color and flavor. (bad for your kidneys and liver and can cause cancer)

Artificial coloring

In Myanmar, many imported food contain banned levels of food dyes which can stain your tongue or cause food poisoning.

Consequences

Increased risk of chronic diseases

Eating food that contains preservatives may increase the risk of chronic diseases because these foods are often highly processed, low in nutrients, and high in salt, sugar, or unhealthy fats.

Weakened immune system

Eating foods that mainly contain MSG without enough nutrients from vegetables and meat can weaken the immune system over time due to poor nutrition, making the body more easy to infect caused by viruses and bacteria.

Digestive Issues

A weak immune system fails to protect the body, leading to frequent infections, slow healing, and chronic fatigue. It can be caused by low nutrient diets.

Monosodium glutamate (MSG)

Having so many food that contain MSG can lead to Headache, Visual aura without headache, Flushing, Body tingling Quick, fluttering heartbeats, called palpitations, Chest pain or heaviness, Feeling sick to the stomach, called nausea. Muscle aches.

Heart disease

Heart disease occurs when the heart and blood vessels are damaged over time, often caused by unhealthy diet, lack of exercise, smoking, and high blood pressure.

Damaged liver and kidneys

Eating unsafe food can cause food poisoning, stomach infections, and digestive problems. Over time, exposure to harmful chemicals may damage organs like the liver and kidneys and affect overall health.
